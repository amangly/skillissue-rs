# Cluely references and brand review

Reviewed 2026-10-19. This records a preliminary U.S.-focused review, not legal
clearance. Distribution countries and development history still need review.

## Verified sources

- [Cluely's terms](https://cluely.com/terms), sections 2.3 and 4.1, reserve rights
  in its materials and restrict customer copying, derivative works, unauthorized
  scraping, and competitor access to the service without consent. Section 11.7
  also restricts publicity and use of the parties' names. These are contractual
  provisions: applicability and enforceability depend on the accepted agreement,
  access history, and applicable law. They do not establish ownership of every
  similar software function.
- [Cluely's privacy policy](https://cluely.com/privacy-policy) identifies the
  service provider as Cluely, Inc.
- The [USPTO's confusion guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/likelihood-confusion)
  considers similarity in sound, appearance, meaning, or commercial impression,
  together with related goods and services. A different implementation language
  does not answer that trademark question.
- [USPTO guidance on registration](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/basics/why-register-your-trademark)
  explains that U.S. trademark rights can arise through use without a federal
  registration. An incomplete registry search is not evidence of no rights.

## Registry limits

application, serial 99160158, filed by Cluely, Inc. on April 28, 2025. Its
description concerns a circle-and-line logo, not a word-only CLUELY mark.
This is a search lead, not a verified current status or a complete mark search.

The [official TSDR record link](https://tsdr.uspto.gov/#caseNumber=99160158&caseSearchType=US_APPLICATION&caseType=DEFAULT&searchType=statusSearch)
did not expose case-specific status through the browsing tool. Do not describe
this filing as registered, abandoned, or otherwise resolved without checking the
live official record. Word marks, other filings, and other countries remain unchecked.
No public brand-use license was established from the official pages reviewed.

## Current project findings

A tracked source/documentation text search found Cluely references in README.md
and AGENTS.md. The README uses the name to explain motivation and workflow
inspiration and includes a non-affiliation notice. The app and installer use the
skillissue name and the maintainer's supplied logo.

This text search does not establish asset ownership, code provenance, or visual
distinctiveness. The supplied logo has not received an independent ownership or
similarity review. Dependency-license coverage is also still incomplete.

## Practical project rules

- Keep skillissue prominent in the app, installer, repository, and release art.
- Use Cluely's name only where needed for an accurate reference or comparison.
  Assess that use in context; the footer does not itself grant permission.
- Preserve the non-affiliation notice. Do not claim official compatibility,
  endorsement, affiliation, or clean-room development without supporting evidence.
- Use the maintainer's authorized artwork and licensed third-party assets.
  Do not import Cluely's logo, wordmark, icons, or promotional artwork.
- Record reference sources and distinguish observed functionality from copied
  code, text, and artwork. Review past service access and accepted terms before
  further research that uses Cluely's service.
- Before commercial distribution, obtain jurisdiction-specific review of name
  clearance for skillissue, logo provenance, overall presentation, and applicable
  contracts. Copyright, patents, and recording/privacy requirements are separate
  questions that this trademark review does not resolve.
